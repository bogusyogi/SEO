## Backlink prospect policy

A catalogue entry is a lead to research, not a recommended placement. Verify current
relevance, audience, listing eligibility, URL, cost, editorial terms and owner
permission. Prefer genuine useful product/company listings, original contributions
and earned citations. Do not auto-submit, invent reviews, mass-post links or claim
a provider authority score is a Google metric. Incomplete snapshots cannot prove loss.
