# Blog production contract

SEO owns the complete search-content workflow without requiring another skill
system. The host may use its writing/design roles, or produce the draft itself.
Use the site's approved facts, author profile, assets and editorial policy.

## Plan before generating

Load the site's goals, query/page ownership, existing content, conversion path
and market. Choose refresh vs new page from evidence. A brief specifies one
primary intent, intended URL, reader, useful original contribution, sources,
outline, internal links, claim risks and evaluation metric. Do not create a new
URL merely because two keyword variants exist.

## Facts and quality

Every substantive factual claim must have an approved source or be removed.
Never invent an interview, first-hand experience, author credential, testimonial,
product feature, price, study or statistic. Missing necessary facts produce a
blocked brief with specific questions, not a fabricated publishable article.
Writing must be reader-first and in the approved brand voice. No mandatory word
count, keyword density, FAQ quota or fixed article length is a Google rule.

## Page anatomy

Use a descriptive title and H1, appropriate direct answer or introduction,
logical headings, accessible images, real author/byline/date, useful navigation
and relevant contextual internal links. A summary or TOC is conditional on the
article, not ritual. Check mobile rendering and the conversion path.

Title/description lengths are preview heuristics, not hard ranking limits.
Canonical and robots directives must match intended indexability. Use per-post
Open Graph and social images with accurate dimensions. Image choices and any
no-stock/competitor-link restrictions belong in per-site policy.

## Structured data and crawler controls

Article/BlogPosting and BreadcrumbList may describe visible appropriate content.
Use Person/Organization identity only when supported by real facts. FAQPage and
HowTo are not universally required, nor does valid Schema.org syntax guarantee
Google rich-result eligibility. Verify current Google support separately; the
schema hook checks syntax and unresolved placeholders, not ranking eligibility.
Do not automatically add noai/noimageai or llms.txt as a Google SEO requirement.
Training controls and search/citation access are separate decisions.

## Execution

Queue an evidence-backed draft. Validate claim sources and JSON-LD, render or
build a preview, and bind approval to the exact draft/change digest. Apply through
an explicitly configured repository or CMS adapter with a baseline, idempotency
key and rollback. The runtime's local patch adapter is not by itself a deployment.
Run a configured deploy adapter and verify the public page separately before
recording deployed verification. Submit URLs only via a supported, explicitly
authorized method; the Google Indexing API is not a general blog-indexing API.

Schedule remeasurement against the baseline after the configured maturity
window. Successful publication is not proof of rankings, conversions or revenue.

## Historical user restrictions

Stunning Strangers was explicitly technical-only: no commercial campaigns,
marketing blog, link acquisition or GEO/AEO promotion. Preserve this restriction
in the site's `.seo/site.yaml` policy (`technical_only: true`) unless the owner
changes it. This reusable skill must not silently override existing site policy.
